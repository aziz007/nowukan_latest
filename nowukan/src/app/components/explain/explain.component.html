1Words Explained

The nowUKan Words section is the first stage of our four stage English development process.

Choose a topic and skill level, then follow the onscreen instructions.

Complete Words to move on to Phrases, where you'll learn how to use words in context.

Save or repeat lessons to build confidence through practice.

2Phrases Explained

The nowUKan Phrases section is the second stage of our four stage development process.

Follow the onscreen instructions. Once completed, you'll automatically move on to Dialogue.

Here, learners see how words come together to form meaningful phrases.

Save or repeat the lesson at the end. Repetition is recommended to build language confidence.

3Dialogue Explained

The nowUKan Dialogue section is the third stage of our four stage development process.

Practise real world dialogue using words and phrases you've already learned, building vocabulary, confidence, and fluency.

Completion leads to QuickFire, where learners test recall, pronunciation, and confidence.

4QuickFire Explained

QuickFire is the fourth stage, testing pronunciation, listening and memory recall.

Hear 10 words or phrases once, then record what you heard in one attempt.

Focused practice builds concentration, confidence and recall.

Save, repeat or share your achievements when you finish.
