1Words Explained

The NowUkan Words section is the first step in our three-stage development process.

Select your topic, choose a skill level matching your current fluency, and follow the onscreen process.

After this section, you move on to Phrases, where words are used in context.

Learners can save or repeat the lesson. Repetition is recommended because practice builds confidence.

2Phrases Explained

The NowUkan Phrases section is the second step in our three-stage development process.

The learner simply follows the onscreen process. Once completed, they automatically move to Dialogue.

This stage shows how words become meaningful phrases.

At the end, learners can save or repeat the lesson, which is recommended.

3Dialogue Explained

The NowUkan Dialogue section is the third step in our three-stage development process.

It expands vocabulary and builds overall language skills, confidence and fluency.

Learners engage in real-world dialogue using words and phrases already practised.

Completion leads into QuickFire, where learners test recall, pronunciation and confidence.

4QuickFire Explained

QuickFire is the fourth stage and tests pronunciation, listening focus and memory recall.

NowUkan presents 10 words or phrases. Learners hear each one once and have one chance to record what they heard.

This creates a focused practice environment that rewards concentration and repetition.

At the end, learners can save, repeat and share achievements. Mistakes are part of learning — practice makes perfect.
